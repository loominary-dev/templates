Whole games to open and take apart, listed under `demos` in `index.json`.

- `platformer` — generated by `tools/make-platformer-demo.py` from Kenney's packs.
- `dungeon` — generated by `tools/make-dungeon-demo.py` from Kenney's packs; the Scene Manager, Top-down Movement, Bullet.
- `crypt` — generated by `tools/make-crypt-demo.py`; the same six rooms on 0x72's 16x16 Dungeon Tileset (CC0).
- `physics` — generated by `tools/make-physics-demo.py`; Box2D, joints, Meld, Fluid Zone, Field.
- `joints` — generated by `tools/make-joints-demo.py`; a scene for each joint of the Box2D solver: hinge, weld, slider, spring, wheel, follow by force, and Meld: a lump that makes whatever it rolls over part of its own body.
- `cascade` — generated by `tools/make-cascade-demo.py`; Box2D and the Fluid together: ramps, conveyor belts (Set the surface speed), a random gravity scale, Show contact points.
- `tank` — generated by `tools/make-tank-demo.py`; the Fluid on the ARCADE solver: water along flat shelves over a weir, carrying and floating arcade bodies.
- `particles` — generated by `tools/make-particles-demo.py`; the Fluid behavior, Emitter, Drain, Field.
- `bunnymark` — generated by `tools/make-bunnymark-demo.py`; how many moving pictures before the frame rate drops.
- `noise` — generated by `tools/make-noise-demo.py`; a native C library (stb_perlin) through the header importer, a behavior and scripts.
