Whole games to open and take apart, listed under `demos` in `index.json`.

- `platformer` — generated by `tools/make-platformer-demo.py` from Kenney's packs.
- `dungeon` — generated by `tools/make-dungeon-demo.py` from Kenney's packs; the Scene Manager, Top-down Movement, Bullet.
- `physics` — generated by `tools/make-physics-demo.py`; Box2D, joints, Attach, Fluid Zone, Field.
- `cascade` — generated by `tools/make-cascade-demo.py`; Box2D and the Fluid together: ramps, conveyor belts (Set the surface speed), a random gravity scale, Show contact points.
- `particles` — generated by `tools/make-particles-demo.py`; the Fluid behaviour, Emitter, Drain, Field.
- `bunnymark` — generated by `tools/make-bunnymark-demo.py`; how many moving pictures before the frame rate drops.
- `noise` — generated by `tools/make-noise-demo.py`; a native C library (stb_perlin) through the header importer, a behaviour and scripts.
